# PRODUCT — WeatherLens

> Inferred from the Phase 4 brief (2026-11-25). There was no separate product interview: the user pinned the product name, audience, palette and direction, and asked for execution without another planning round.

**Product:** WeatherLens — Logistics Weather Risk Intelligence.

**Users:** business analysts at a US logistics company, choosing which distribution hubs should get resilience investment. They work at desks, in daylight, on large screens. Mobile use is occasional and functional.

**Job to be done:**
- See which hubs are most exposed to weather hazards and why.
- Compare hubs.
- Ask follow-up questions in plain language.
- Trust every number: where it came from, how fresh it is, and how complete it is.

**Truth constraints:**
- Every score, ranking and statistic comes from the backend's deterministic engine.
- The index measures heuristic weather-hazard *exposure*, not disruption probability.
- The hubs are illustrative.
- Partial data is shown, never hidden, and never ranked as if it were comparable.
- AI answers can take 5–45 s, and there is no streaming.

**Surface mode:** Operate (analytical app). Methodology & Data is a Read section inside it.

**Pinned direction:**
- Navy, slate and teal, on neutral backgrounds.
- Restrained amber and red, used only for exposure levels.
- Modern sans typography.
- Professional tables and cards.
- Desktop-first.
- No excessive gradients, oversized headings, decorative illustrations or gratuitous animation.

**Anti-references:** generic chatbot UI; coding-assignment look; marketing-hero dashboards.
